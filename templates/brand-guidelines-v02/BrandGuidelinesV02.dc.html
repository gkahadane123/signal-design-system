02

One brand idea: better intelligence

01 · Foundation

The future will not be won by better AI alone. It will be won by better intelligence — everything we make should feel like the product: confident, precise, quietly warm.

Lead with the shift

Frame the market reality first, then position Intelligence Node as the foundation. The urgency is real; don't inflate it.

Precise over promotional

Specific, verified figures beat adjectives. "99% exact-match accuracy," never "industry-leading."

Intelligence is the through-line

Every message ladders back to the core idea: real-time, trusted intelligence for human and AI decisions.

03

Write like an analyst, not an advertiser

02 · Voice & tone
We say

"Match similar and identical products with 99% accuracy."

"The intelligence layer powering agentic retail."

"Real-time, trusted intelligence for human and AI decisions."

We avoid

"Revolutionary, game-changing AI" — hype stacking.

"Data provider" and "pricing tool" — undersells the layer.

Exclamation marks, emoji, unverified numbers, buzzword stacks.

Sentence case everywhere, including headlines and buttons. Second person "you" for the customer; "we" sparingly. Tone dial: social — confident with a point of view · decks — declarative and quantified · product & email — plain and brief.

04

The dots stay exactly as they are

03 · Logo

Primary. Full-color on Paper or Surface. Default everywhere.

Reversed. White version on Ink, Astronaut, Reef, or coral only — never mid-tone backgrounds.

05

Clear space, minimum size, misuse

03 · Logo
Clear space

Equal to the dot-cluster radius on all sides. Nothing enters this zone.

Minimum size

Digital: 120px wide. Print: 30mm. Below that, use the dot sphere alone.

Never

Rebuild or recolor the dots · stretch or rotate · add shadows · place on mid-tone or busy backgrounds.

07

Paper leads. Coral marks the action.

04 · Color
Usage

Paper is the primary background for every page, screen, and slide. Surface is only for cards and panels sitting on Paper. All UI tints — borders, hovers, muted text — derive from the eight colors via opacity. If a layout feels "coral," it's wrong: coral marks the one thing the eye should land on.

Accessibility
Ink on Paper — body text · 15.95:1 White on coralbuttons · ≥14px medium, never body copy Paper on Astronaut / Reef / Ink — AAA Never: coral paragraphs · coral small text on Paper (2.49:1) · text on photos without a scrim

09

Real retail, natural light, warm grade

06 · Imagery
photo: buyers & merchandisers at work

Subject. People in real decision contexts. No stock handshakes, no glowing AI brains, no rendered people.

photo + ink scrim 50–65%

Treatment. Ink scrim at 50–65% whenever Paper text sits on a photo. Subtle warm grade to sit with Paper.

photo aligned to grid column

Placement. Photos align to grid columns; one coral edge may tag an image. Never a coral tint over the whole photo.

Designed to coexist with what exists
