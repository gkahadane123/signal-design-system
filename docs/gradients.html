Each of the six themes is named by mood and carries one area of Intelligence Node’s work. They are built in CSS from layered radial blooms plus a grain texture. The grain is mandatory: it is a large part of why the gradients read as premium.

Using them

  • Apply a class, .sig-grad-ember and so on, to a section, tile or panel. They work best as full-bleed section backgrounds.
  • Dark themes (Ember, Depth, Current, Night) take Paper text. Light themes (Bloom, Glow) take Ink.
  • Anchor them in the grid. Put a gradient inside a frame, a tile, a wash or a section, never as a free-floating blob.
  • Do not rebuild them ad hoc. The classes are the source of truth; the 2400px PNG masters in assets/gradients/ are for print and design-tool image fills.
<section class="sig-grad-night" style="color: var(--paper)">
  <h2 class="t-display-2">Decision</h2>
</section>

The extended hues

The gradients use a wider set of hues than the palette. Those hues live only inside gradients. They are never standalone swatches, interface colors or text colors.
