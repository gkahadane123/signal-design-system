Cards, tags and verified stats.

Card

Card container — Surface on Paper with hairline + warm shadow.

<Card><h3 className="t-title">Pricing intelligence</h3><p className="t-small">…</p></Card>
<Card variant="dark" interactive onClick={open}>…</Card>

radius defaults to 16px; use var(--radius-card-sm) (12px) for small cards.

Stat

Big verified number with its citation — use ONLY Claims-Register-verified figures.

<Stat value="+4,700%" label="YoY gen-AI traffic to US retail sites" source="Adobe Analytics, July 2025" />
<Stat value="190,000+" label="brands" size="sm" />
