Neue Haas Grotesk Display Pro is the only typeface in the system. There is no serif, no monospace and no secondary face. Inter appears only as a fallback in the font stack.

The family

Headings use weight 600 at 28px and above. Body copy uses weight 400. The other cuts are available for special cases.

Mechanics

  • Sentence case everywhere, including headlines and buttons. Only the tiny eyebrow labels are uppercase.
  • Numbers use tabular figures. Add .t-data to any column or readout of numbers.
  • Hierarchy is weight and size. Do not reach for color or a second face to create emphasis.
Do

Use weight 600 for headings at 28px and larger.

Don’t

Set a heading in a light weight at small sizes, or mix in a serif for contrast.
