The logo

The mark is a dot-density sphere, coral dots aggregating into a signal, set beside the wordmark. Use the supplied files; never rebuild the dots.

  • Clear space equals the radius of the dot cluster, on all sides.
  • Never on mid-tone backgrounds. Use the color lockup on light grounds and the white lockup on dark ones.
  • On slides, the monogram sits top-left of content slides, and the full lockup bookends covers and closings.

Photography

Use real human photography: buyers, merchandisers, and store and ecommerce teams in real decision contexts. Documentary, candid, natural light, with a subtle warm grade so it sits with Paper. Align photos to grid columns.
