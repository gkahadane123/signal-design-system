Signal holds the decisions that make a product screen, a sales deck, a web page and a social post look and sound like the same company: color, type, spacing, gradients, icons, components and voice. It exists so that nobody has to re-decide any of them.

What it designs for

Intelligence Node is the intelligence layer at the center of the digital shelf: real-time, trusted retail intelligence that powers human and AI decisions. The design system follows from that position. It is confident and precise, it leads with data, and it never reaches for hype.

The future will not be won by better AI alone. It will be won by better intelligence.

The functional promise is three short sentences, and a good screen delivers them in order: see change, understand impact, act with confidence.

Ground rules

Six rules hold everywhere. Each is explained on its own page, and each is the thing most often broken by accident.

  1. Paper is the ground. Every page, screen and slide sits on Paper. Surface is for cards and controls that sit on top of it. Color
  2. Coral is for the one action. It is a fill and a display color, never small text, and the label on a coral fill is white. Color
  3. One typeface. Neue Haas Grotesk Display Pro, with hierarchy from weight and size alone. Typography
  4. Tints, not new colors. Borders, hovers and muted text are the eight palette colors at lower opacity. Color
  5. Icons start at 48px. The set is drawn for 48, 64 and 96. Iconography
  6. Sentence case, no emoji. Short declarative sentences, with numbers that are verified and cited. Voice and content
