Intelligence Node speaks with the authority of its data, with empathy balanced by candor. The voice is confident and precise. It does not chase trends.

Three principles

  1. Lead with the shift, not the sell. Frame the market reality first, then position Intelligence Node as the foundation. The urgency is real; do not inflate it.
  2. Precise over promotional. Specific, verified figures beat adjectives. “99% exact-match accuracy”, never “industry-leading”.
  3. Intelligence is the through-line. Every message ladders back to real-time, trusted intelligence for human and AI decisions.

Mechanics

  • Sentence case everywhere, including headlines and buttons.
  • Second person, “you”, for the customer. “We” sparingly.
  • Short declarative sentences: See change. Understand impact. Act with confidence.
  • No emoji. No exclamation marks. Unicode arrows (→) are fine in links and calls to action.

Numbers

Every number published externally is verified and cited in scope, with the source named exactly. In the product and in decks, the Stat component enforces this by requiring a source line next to the figure.

No customer names or logos appear in brand materials unless their use has been explicitly cleared.
