Layout in Signal is built on a hairline grid. The 32px line grid is the brand’s structural substrate: content and gradients align to its columns, and technical framing such as ticks and crosshairs hangs off it.

The grid

  • Container: 1200px, with a 760px narrow container for reading.
  • Gutter: 24px.
  • Grid unit: 32px, drawn as a hairline grid in Ink at 4% opacity (Paper at 4% in dark mode), so it sits behind the content instead of competing with it. Grid lines are neutral, never coral.

Paper and Surface

Paper is the page. Surface is reserved for elevated things that sit on Paper: cards, panels, inputs and dialogs. Surface is never a page or band background. For full-bleed variety, use the line grid on Paper, or a dark band in Ink, Astronaut or Reef with Paper text.

Radius, borders and shadow

ElementRadius
ButtonsFully rounded pill, --radius-btn: 999px

Borders are hairlines, Ink at 12%. Shadows are warm and subtle: cards use --shadow-card (0 1px 2px and 0 8px 24px, both Ink at a few percent), popovers use the deeper --shadow-pop. There are no inner shadows, and no glassmorphism as a motif.
