Motion in Signal is restrained. The signature is a light pulse that travels along the grid lines, slow and sparse enough to read as intelligence at work rather than decoration. Floating particle-mesh backgrounds are explicitly rejected.

Timing and easing

TokenValue

--dur-fast120ms

--dur-slow480ms

Reduced motion

Always honor prefers-reduced-motion. With motion off, the static grid remains and nothing is lost.

Hover, press and focus

  • Prefer non-color cues. Hovers are Ink washes at 4 to 8%, and link underlines thicken.
  • Primary button: darkens with an Ink 8% overlay on hover and 12% on press. Ghost buttons darken their white fill the same way.
  • Focus: a 2px Aqua ring, offset 2px, on every interactive element.
